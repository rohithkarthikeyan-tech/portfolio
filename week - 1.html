Week 1 – 5S Activity and Portfolio Development

During the first week of the internship, we participated in a 5S workplace organization activity for the first three days. I was assigned to the Cables Team, where our responsibility was to sort and organize different types of cables into separate labeled boxes. We completed most of this work on the first day by identifying, grouping, and arranging the cables systematically.

On the second day, after completing the major cable-sorting tasks, I assisted the DFAB Team with their organization activities whenever required while also supporting my own team. This helped me understand the importance of teamwork and collaboration in maintaining an organized workspace.

On the third day, our team arranged the organized cable boxes neatly inside the storage cabinet, ensuring they were easily accessible and properly labeled. This activity demonstrated the practical application of the 5S principles, resulting in a cleaner, more efficient, and well-organized work environment.

During the remaining two days of the week, we began developing our personal portfolios. We worked on presenting our educational background, technical skills, projects, achievements, and career objectives in a professional format. This activity helped us improve our self-presentation skills and prepare for future career opportunities.

Overall, the first week enhanced my understanding of workplace organization, teamwork, and professional profile development while emphasizing the importance of maintaining an efficient and organized work environment.
