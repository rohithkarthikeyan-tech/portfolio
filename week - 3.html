Week 3

During Week 3, we learned the basics of electrical and electronic systems on Monday and Tuesday. We were introduced to fundamental electrical components, circuits, and their applications.

On Wednesday and Thursday, we learned the basics of Fusion 360, including the different tools and options available for 3D modelling and design. In the evening, I designed a tool kit using toy clay to understand the modelling process. On Thursday evening, we also made paper rockets and conducted a competition to see whose rocket travelled the longest distance. There were two rounds: the first was completed without using a phone, while the second round allowed us to use a phone for assistance.

At the end of the week, we were given an assignment that included modelling the clay tool kit and paper rocket, along with designing a new part using Fusion 360. On Friday, we presented our designs and underwent an evaluation of the completed assignment.
