Week 4

During the fourth week of our Forge training, we gained practical experience in CAD designing, digital fabrication, 3D printing, teamwork, and communication. On Monday, we worked on a random water bottle model and recreated its design using Fusion 360. During the modelling process, we learned how to use the Joint option to establish movement between different components. We applied this to the bottle cap so that the cap could be opened and closed, which helped us understand how joints and assembly movements work in Fusion 360. On Tuesday, we were introduced to the laser cutting process. We selected a logo image from Google and worked on converting the image into a suitable digital format and then into a DXF file. The DXF file was imported into RDWorks, where we adjusted and finalized the logo design so that it could be prepared for laser cutting. This helped us understand the workflow involved in converting an image into a format suitable for digital fabrication.

On Wednesday, we learned how to animate parts and assemblies in Fusion 360. We explored how different components can be given movement and how the interaction between parts can be represented through animation. Some students also attended the evaluation for the assignments given during the previous week. On Thursday, we were introduced to 3D printing and learned about the basic workflow involved in converting a digital design into a physical object. We installed Bambu Studio and learned how to import a design, prepare it for printing, and generate the required printing setup before sending it to the 3D printer. On Friday, we were selected for the Alpha Team, and I was assigned to a team where I would be working as a Designer. This gave me an opportunity to take responsibility for design-related activities and work together with other team members. On Saturday, we focused on completing the assignments given for the week. We also participated in a debate on the topic of influencers, where we discussed arguments both in favour of and against influencers and their impact on society. Overall, Week 4 was a productive and practical week that helped me improve my knowledge of Fusion 360 modelling and animation, laser cutting, 3D printing, digital fabrication, teamwork, design thinking, communication, and presentation skills.
