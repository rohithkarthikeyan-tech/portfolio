Week 0 – Orientation Program

The zeroth week of the Forge internship began with an orientation session conducted by our mentors. They introduced us to Forge, its vision, mission, and the overall structure of the internship. We learned about the program objectives, learning process, expectations, and how Forge promotes innovation and entrepreneurship through problem-solving, design thinking, teamwork, and user-centered solutions.

During the orientation, we completed the 16Personalities (MBTI) assessment to understand our strengths and working styles. My personality type was Advocate (INFJ), which reflects qualities such as creativity, empathy, integrity, and a strong sense of purpose. The assessment helped me better understand my personality and areas for personal and professional growth.

We were also introduced to Zen Pencils, which shares inspiring life lessons through illustrated stories based on the experiences of successful people. These sessions emphasized the importance of perseverance, self-belief, continuous learning, and maintaining a positive mindset while overcoming challenges.

Overall, the orientation week provided a strong foundation for the internship by helping us understand the Forge ecosystem, discover our strengths, and prepare for the learning journey ahead.
