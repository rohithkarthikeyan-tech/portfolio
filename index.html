FSAE Chassis Engineering

Formula Student Chassis Development

Project Overview & Objectives

As part of Team Sakthi Racing's Chassis Department, I designed and optimized the tubular spaceframe chassis for a Formula Student race car. The primary goal was to achieve high structural torsional rigidity while minimizing overall vehicle mass and ensuring smooth engine packaging.

Technical Accomplishments & Innovations

  • Weight Savings: Successfully reduced chassis overall weight by ~500 grams through targeted tube wall thickness selection and triangulation optimization.
  • Pull-Bar Suspension Hardpoints: Integrated custom mounting points designed specifically for a pull-bar suspension layout.
  • Engine Envelope: Improved engine compartment spacing, simplifying installation, maintenance access, and intake/exhaust routing.
  • Manufacturing Fixtures: Introduced custom metal fixtures and notch templates during fabrication to eliminate distortion during TIG/MIG welding and boost frame alignment accuracy.
