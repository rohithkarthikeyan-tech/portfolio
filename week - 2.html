Week 2 Summary

This week focused on improving our knowledge of innovation, programming, and mobile application development. On Monday, we attended an expert session by Mukesh, an accomplished author who has written and published several books, including Lean Spark. He shared valuable insights on innovation, entrepreneurship, and product development.

On Tuesday, our mentor introduced us to algorithms, flowcharts, and the basics of Python programming, helping us understand logical problem-solving and coding fundamentals.

On Wednesday, we explored Scratch, a block-based programming platform that can be used to create interactive projects such as animations, games, and memes. This session improved our understanding of visual programming.

On Thursday, we learned to use MIT App Inventor for Android application development. We identified a real-world problem and began designing and developing an Android application (APK) to address it.

On Friday, the Vice President of Forge conducted a session on Applied Design Thinking, where we learned practical approaches to solving real-world problems through innovation. We concluded the week by updating our internship portfolio with the knowledge and work completed during the week.
